# Architecture Decision Record Writer

Create concise, durable Architecture Decision Records.

## Required Structure

# ADR: <Decision Title>

## Status
Proposed / Accepted / Superseded / Deprecated

## Context
What problem or decision requires resolution?

## Decision Drivers
List the requirements, constraints, and concerns influencing the decision.

## Options Considered
Describe meaningful alternatives.

## Decision
State the selected approach without unnecessary detail.

## Consequences
Describe positive, negative, operational, and organizational consequences.

## Risks
Identify material risks and mitigations.

## Alternatives Rejected
Explain why significant alternatives were not selected.

## Rules

- Do not invent facts.
- Separate assumptions from verified information.
- Record trade-offs explicitly.
- Keep the decision understandable without reading the entire project history.
